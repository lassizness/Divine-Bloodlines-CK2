Divine Bloodlines v2.1.6.8.1 — Player Founder Start Host Hotfix

PLAYER START ARMIES
- A player-selected Zeus now receives a one-time 2,000-man zero-maintenance, non-reinforcing host in the player's current capital during founder initialization.
- Zeus composition matches auto-arrival Zeus exactly: 500 light infantry, 700 heavy infantry, 350 archers, 250 pikemen, 150 light cavalry, 50 knights.
- A player-selected Odin now receives a one-time 2,000-man zero-maintenance, non-reinforcing host in the player's current capital during founder initialization.
- Odin composition matches auto-arrival Odin exactly: 650 light infantry, 800 heavy infantry, 300 archers, 150 light cavalry, 100 knights.
- Auto-arrival founders are explicitly excluded, so they continue to receive only their existing Thessalia/Uppland arrival host.
- db_player_start_host_received prevents duplicate spawning if founder initialization is ever retriggered.

COMPATIBILITY
- No changes to auto-arrival timing, title grants, divine genetics, Sickle, Legendary Gathering, Order of the Sickle, or death protection.
- EN/RU gameplay scripts are synchronized.
