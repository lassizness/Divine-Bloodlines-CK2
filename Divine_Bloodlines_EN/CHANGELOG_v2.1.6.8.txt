Divine Bloodlines v2.1.6.8 — Divine Systems / Legendary / Sickle / Genetics

LEGENDARY GATHERING / WARRIOR LODGES
- Fixed the apparent Legendary Gathering deadlock for a true god at Hero rank IV.
- A rank-IV true god now repairs the vanilla Holy Fury requirement for two accessible Heroes by promoting an available AI lodge veteran; the repair runs monthly while legend progress is active.
- Existing saves with a stuck full progress bar can recover through the same maintenance path.
- Restored is_member_of_any_warrior_lodge_trigger to vanilla ownership; Divine Bloodlines no longer globally overrides this membership helper.
- Intact adult gods cannot be maimed by the generic duel injury resolver; duel damage is capped at wounded.
- During a Legendary Journey, an intact true god entering top bracket is raised to at least progress 10 so the vanilla AI-only 10% imprisonment/failure branch cannot remove the god from the journey.

CRONUS' SICKLE / GODSLAYER
- Sickle challenge target scope is now worldwide (filter = all) and launches a real Holy Fury duel.
- A non-Godslayer Sickle wielder uses a two-stage duel rule against a true god:
  1) a strong winning lethal-quality result against an intact adult god severs immortality instead of killing;
  2) a later strong winning result against the severed god calls the canonical Sickle finishing blow and can create the first Godslayer bloodline.
- Existing Godslayer descendants keep their separate right to kill intact gods directly, even if they also carry the Sickle.
- The inherited Godslayer Challenge is now a real duel instead of the old 55/80% instant-kill roll.
- Prisoner judgment remains a special captive execution path for Sickle/Godslayer holders.

ORDER OF THE SICKLE
- Added an Order Codex decision explaining the Order, the Sickle, intelligence, ranks and the Grand Hunt.
- Added a target dossier decision for divine characters with descriptions for intelligence levels 0–4.
- Grand Hunt Godslayer resolution now uses the real Godslayer duel rather than an instant random kill.
- Existing v2.1.6.6 population controller remains: normal recruitment stops around 45, emergency ceiling 60.

DIVINE ARTIFACT SEARCH
- Normal relic searches are now delayed expeditions (60–180 days) rather than immediate silent random rolls.
- Added explicit FOUND / FAILED / ALREADY EXISTS result events.
- Successful search: 5-year cooldown; failure: 3-year cooldown; relic already in the world: 1-year cooldown.
- Existing unique-relic registry remains authoritative and prevents duplicates.

DIVINE JUDGMENT
- Zeus Thunderbolt mortal death chance reduced to 20%.
- Gungnir mortal death chance reduced to 18%.
- Active demigod death chance reduced to 8%.
- Intact true gods cannot be killed by these direct divine-strike powers.
- Added Divine Judgment Survivor trait: +1 health, +1 martial, +10 personal combat, +0.15 monthly prestige.
- A previous survivor has only a 5% death chance from a later Thunderbolt/Gungnir strike.
- Added anonymous death reasons death_divine_judgment / death_divine_spear. These deaths do not set killer = ROOT, so the victim history does not identify/click-link the divine attacker as an ordinary murderer.

DIVINE GENETICS 2.0
- Added hidden ancestry essences for Zeus, Odin, Poseidon, Hades, Demeter and Hestia. Ancestry is now distinct from current blood strength.
- Added Cross-Pantheon God as a true-god state. Two full gods no longer automatically produce a demigod.
- Full Norse + full Olympian unions produce a cross-pantheon true god while preserving both ancestry essences.
- Full Olympian + full Olympian unions remain true gods and preserve all relevant Olympian essences.
- Added Mixed Demigod Blood for diluted descendants of cross-pantheon gods; exact ancestry remains in hidden essence traits.
- Zeus/Odin active-blood helpers recognize their essence in cross-pantheon and mixed descendants.
- Blood of Two Thrones remains a separate high convergence state and is not granted merely by being born to two gods.
- Added old-save migration for essence markers and old full-god children incorrectly represented as Stormrune/Fate-Thunder/Two-Pantheons demigods.

HEROES / AGE OF HEROES
- Heroic Path no longer fires automatically at age 16.
- A demigod becomes eligible from age 20 with meaningful prestige or attributes; yearly offer chance is 25%.
- Heroic Path choices now have distinct descriptions/bonuses and start with a 2-year trial cooldown.
- Restored the yearly Heroic Trial controller: 12% before the Age of Heroes, 18% after it.
- Heroic Trials are counted. Hero Cult now requires age 30, 3000 prestige and at least two completed trials.
- Age of Heroes cannot begin before 15 years after the first divine arrival and requires at least 10 adult active demigods plus 3 established heroic demigods.
- The Age of Heroes modifier is applied to the adult demigod generation rather than only the event ROOT.
- World-state check can run from divine founders in observer games as well as human player roots.

COMPATIBILITY
- age >= 16 remains the exact start of adult intact true-god death protection.
- No static manifestation landed titles were reintroduced.
- New traits were appended; old trait order was not rearranged.
- EN/RU gameplay logic is synchronized; language-specific founder names/localisation remain separate.
