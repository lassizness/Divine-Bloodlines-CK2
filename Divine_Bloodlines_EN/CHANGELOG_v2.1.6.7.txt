Divine Bloodlines v2.1.6.7 — Death Guard / Duel Hotfix

CORE RULE
- Full gods younger than 16 remain vulnerable as before.
- Adult intact full gods (age >= 16, immortality not severed) cannot be killed by ordinary mortals or ordinary animal/accident paths covered by the vanilla helpers below.
- Active Godslayer blood remains a valid exception and may kill an intact adult full god.
- Gods whose immortality was severed by the Sickle remain normally mortal.

DUELS
- Added a late override of Holy Fury send_duel_result_event_effect.
- All lethal random-result branches are now victim-aware:
  * double death;
  * attacker death;
  * defender death;
  * battlefield lethal/prison result;
  * legendary-lodge attacker death;
  * legendary-lodge defender death;
  * legendary-lodge double death.
- A lethal branch involving a protected god is excluded unless the opposing combatant has active Godslayer blood.
- Fixed HFP.10105 "kill them anyway" protection when the actual loser is stored in event_target:other_combatant rather than combatant_2.
- Existing non-lethal duel outcomes are preserved.

ANIMALS / INJURY CANDIDATE SAFETY
- Added a vanilla-compatible can_be_maimed_trigger override: protected adult intact gods are not valid ordinary maiming candidates.
- This blocks many vanilla hunting, feast, battle, job and random-event paths before they can select a god for a dangerous result.
- Extended the existing one-day animal safety scrubber to clear lethal duel carry-over flags and reassert immortality/diplomatic immunity.
- Clears queued HFP.10102 / HF.12205 loser events when the protected god itself is the delayed target.
- Existing tiger/wolf/eagle/bear/lion hunting target flags continue to be scrubbed.

EXISTING GUARDS RETAINED
- Standard targeted duels and post-duel executions.
- Battlefield duel helper / Indomitable protection.
- Standard assassination and murder-plot guards.
- Random accident helper guard.
- Prison escape and hardcoded execution-interaction guard.

COMPATIBILITY
- No vanilla event IDs are duplicated by this patch.
- No events/ replace_path is used.
- The patch overrides only specific scripted helpers late in load order, preserving normal vanilla behavior for non-gods.
