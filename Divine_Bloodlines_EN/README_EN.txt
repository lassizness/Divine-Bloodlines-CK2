Divine Bloodlines v2.1.6.2 — CK2 3.3.5.1 / Holy Fury

Installation:
1. Copy Divine_Bloodlines_EN and Divine_Bloodlines_EN.mod into Documents\Paradox Interactive\Crusader Kings II\mod\.
2. Enable only the English package.
3. A new campaign is recommended for clean testing; a migration event handles the main divine states in 2.1.1 saves.

Highlights:
- Blood inheritance follows biological parents: paternal blood from the father, maternal blood from the mother. Marriage, concubinage and bastardy do not override biology.
- True gods arise only when the required divine halves actually meet. Demigods remain mortal but long-lived and now receive heroic destinies and recurring trials.
- True-god status covers Zeus, Odin, their full descendants, the converged two-thrones god and independent Olympian gods.
- Cronus' Sickle no longer appears randomly. It is a unique lost relic found through a search chain. The first true god killed with it founds the Godslayer Bloodline, whose descendants gain a Sickle-free God Hunt action.
- Capturing a true god grants the God-Captor trait. Gods and demigods have enhanced prison-escape mechanics.
- Zeus' animal forms use static animal portraits without changing culture, DNA or the original human face.
- Odin has expanded active gameplay: ravens, runes, Gungnir, sacred oaths, blessings, Valkyries, Draupnir, Mead of Poetry and Sleipnir; the Wanderer and Yggdrasil chains remain.
- Zeus and Odin receive long-term religious restoration/reformation goals, switching to faith expansion after reformation.
- New world events cover demigod births, new true gods, reawakened blood, divine population milestones and the first Godslayer.

Important: CK2 itself is not installed in the build environment. The archive is statically validated but has not been launched in the game client. Fresh error.log/game.log files are useful after the first test.

v2.1.4 additions:
- If Zeus or Odin was not selected by a player, that missing founder can descend into the world once during the first few campaign years. A selected founder consumes the same one-time slot; dead founders never respawn.
- The Order of the Sickle remains an active secret AI society with surveillance, demigod hunts, relic theft, traps, Sickle tracing and Grand Hunts.
- Every true god receives personal Sickle warnings, while world events announce automatic Zeus/Odin arrivals.
- True gods and active demigods now exert religion-independent divine allure through vanilla general-opinion and sex-appeal mechanics.
- Mortal-vs-god protection is reinforced before the first combat pulse and on prisoner execution attempts; existing plot/Assassinate/duel/battle guards remain.


v2.1.5 additions:
- Zeus and Odin automatic arrivals create fresh characters instead of overwriting existing rulers; Odin cannot arrive as somebody's spiritual adviser and Zeus cannot arrive as a patriarch.
- Divine Attraction is passive only (opinion/sex appeal): there are no automatic romance popups or remote romance actions.
- Divine Union is a narrow manual bypass only for the interfaith marriage block when one side is a true god or active demigod; ordinary mortal interfaith marriage remains unchanged.
- The Order of the Sickle AI uses player-equivalent costs, cooldowns and success rolls; Sickle theft/search and intelligence ageing are corrected.
- Independent Olympian gods produce an Olympian demigod/faded/latent blood line. True goddesses retain limited late-age fertility with population-safe yearly chances.
- Searchable mythic relics are unique campaign-wide; Zeus/Odin line-head succession and several maintenance paths are hardened.

v2.1.6 additions:
- On automatic arrival Zeus immediately receives the County of Thessalia and Odin the County of Uppland. Both are forced independent as territorial rulers.
- The temporary landless Manifestation of Olympus/Asgard titles are only bootstrap titles and are destroyed after the county is secured.
- Each auto-arrived founder receives exactly 2,000 one-time event troops with zero maintenance and no reinforcement for the first conquests.
- Early v2.1.5 saves migrate automatically on the next monthly pulse: a previously landless auto-arrived founder gets the proper county, independence and the one-time 2,000-man host.
- World arrival events now explicitly announce the seized county and starting army.


v2.1.6.2 hotfix:
- Ruler Designer Zeus/Odin founder startup no longer depends on Chronicle: universal on_startup plus a monthly fallback are used.
- Automatic arrivals use a global controller and yearly watchdog, so a lost delayed event cannot permanently suppress the gods.
- Added the missing Olympian-blood trait icons and hardened Thessalia/Uppland establishment.
