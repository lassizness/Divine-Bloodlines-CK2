Divine Bloodlines v2.1.6.8.2

DEATH GUARD LOAD-ORDER FIX
- Renamed vanilla helper overrides from zz_db_* to 00_db_* so CK2 loads Divine Bloodlines definitions before conflicting vanilla 00_/01_ helpers.
- The central send_duel_result_event_effect guard is now actually eligible to become the active definition.
- Ordinary mortals may duel gods; only illegal lethal outcomes are blocked.
- Removed the old battlefield entry override that prevented god-vs-mortal battlefield duels entirely.

DIRECT DUEL DEATH GUARDS
- Added a common last-line resolver for legacy scripted duel deaths.
- Godslayer blood can still kill an intact adult true god.
- The Sickle severs immortality instead of bypassing the first-hit severing stage.
- Added direct protection for Holmgang, regency duel, WoL lover duels, Grand Bloodline Chronicle duel, Kowtow bridge duel, Monastic duel, Child of Destiny attacker death, battlefield surrender execution, Legendary double-death, Reaper suicide duel, seclusion intruder duel and old feast brawls.

SAVE SAFETY
- The one-day divine scrubber now clears additional stale delayed lethal duel events after installing the patch into an existing save.
- age >= 16 remains the adult protection threshold. Severed gods and full-god children remain mortal by design.

Additional final-line hardening:
- HF.10104 claim-duel self-death is unavailable to an adult intact god unless the opponent has active Godslayer blood.
- HFP.10111 direct mercy-kill finalizer now resolves through the divine lethal guard.
- A Sickle finishing blow on an already-severed true god in a legacy direct-duel chain now creates the first Godslayer bloodline if necessary.
